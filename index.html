How the outlier score works. TikTok: a post's views divided by the median views of the account's latest ~20 posts. Instagram: likes against the median likes of the latest 12 posts, since hashtag results don't return views.

Who made the list. Accounts under 50k followers needed 3x or more. Accounts over 50k only got in at 10x or more. All 40 small-account outliers are here, plus the 10 strongest from bigger accounts.
